VELORA HEAT

Velora Bay, golden hour. Reno Karsa owes Damar Wibowo — time to work.

Open world · 6 missions · traffic & pedestrians · wanted 1–5 · 4 weapons · garage & save

WASD move / drive · Mouse aim · Click fire · E interact / steal · R reload · 1–4 weapons · M map · Esc pause · Shift run

How to Play

Walk with WASD (Shift to run). Press E near a car to steal it and drive with W/A/S/D. Follow the gold marker: six missions from Sari Lestari will pay off Reno's debt to Damar Wibowo. Crimes raise your VBPD heat stars — break line of sight to lose them, hide at the Dermaga hideout, or pay $300 for a spray job at Karsa Auto Works. Store cars at the garage (E near it) and they wait for you after a reload. M opens the full city map. Esc pauses — save there, and Continue brings you back.
